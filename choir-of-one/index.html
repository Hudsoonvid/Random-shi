Choir of One

Load a song, sing each of its notes once, and every note you sang gets pasted into place to build the whole song out of your voice.

1. The song

MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) or MIDI (.mid)

Or type the notes

Write notes like C4 D4 E4:2 F#4:.5 R:1. The number after the colon is the length in beats (1 if left out), R is a rest, C4+E4+G4 is a chord. A line with only --- starts another voice from the beginning.

2. Your notes

You hear the note first, then sing it and hold it until the bar fills. If the notes are too high or low, shift the key above (−12 drops an octave).

3. The collage

Filled blocks are your voice. Outlined ones aren't recorded yet.
