Choir of One

Load a song, sing each instrument's part one at a time while the notes scroll past, and all your takes get layered in time into the whole song.

1. The song

MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) or MIDI (.mid)

Or type the notes

Write notes like C4 D4 E4:2 F#4:.5 R:1. The number after the colon is the length in beats (1 if left out), R is a rest, C4+E4+G4 is a chord. A line with only --- starts the next instrument from the beginning.

2. Sing each instrument

Wear headphones so the guide doesn't leak into your take. Record gives you a four-beat count-in, then follow the big note and the moving line. "Octave" moves a part to where you can sing it.
